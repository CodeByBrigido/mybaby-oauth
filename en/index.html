My Baby: Time Capsule is a digital diary designed to record, organize, and preserve your child's childhood memories.

The application allows you to store photos, videos, stories, important moments, and other memories in a digital time capsule, organized to follow your child's journey throughout the years.

Storage in Google Drive

My Baby uses Google Drive to store the files and data required for the time capsule to function. The data is stored in the Google account selected by the user.

The application uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. This permission allows My Baby to work with the files and data used by the application without requesting general access to all of the content in your Google Drive.

The user retains control over their Google account and the data stored in Google Drive. For detailed information about data collection, use, storage, sharing, and deletion, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
