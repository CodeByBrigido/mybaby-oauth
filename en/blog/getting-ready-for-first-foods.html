Getting ready for first foods

What to set aside at home and how to record it. When to start and what to offer is for your paediatrician to say.

This is not health advice

When to start, what to offer and in what order is a conversation with your paediatrician, and only with them. What is here is the practical part: what to have ready at home and how to keep the memory of this stage, which is short and very funny.

What makes life easier

  • A highchair you can wash all over, or a large sheet of plastic underneath.
  • A long-sleeved bib. The small ones are useless at this stage.
  • A suction plate, which buys you a few days before it becomes a toy.
  • Two spoons: one for her to hold and one for you to use.

The memories worth having

  • The face at each new flavour. Photograph the face, not the plate.
  • A short video of the first day sitting in the highchair.
  • The list of foods she loved and the ones she spat out. Fifteen years from now, comparing it with her taste is hilarious.
