Newborn photo ideas at home with a phone

Five easy photos to take in the first weeks, using a phone and the light you already have at home.

Five newborn pictures you can take at home

  • A close-up by the window. Ask someone to hold the baby in a spot with soft daylight. Turn off the flash and tap the face on your screen to focus.
  • Their hand around your finger. Take a close-up so you can see how small they were next to you.
  • A family photo. Prop the phone securely at eye level, set the timer and get in the picture while someone holds the baby.
  • The room as it was. Back up enough to include the chair where you spent the mornings or the book you were reading aloud.
  • A detail you see every day. Try the swirl of hair, a tiny ear or an expression that always makes you smile.

Use the light and take two versions

Try a window with indirect daylight. Take a close-up and a wider picture while you’re in the same spot. Later, the close-up will show the detail and the wider shot will help you remember where you were and who was there.

Add a caption while you remember

Choose a favorite and write down the baby’s age, who took the photo or a detail about that day. One sentence is enough for your first entry in a baby memory book.

Keep it in My Baby

Take the photo and it goes onto the timeline at the right age, kept in the child's Google Drive.
